## Superseded / corrected claims

- **2026-08-29**: An external review (Codex) found that `results/ranking.csv`
  previously contained 174 rows instead of 164, because `combined_view`
  merged on `hip_name` (not unique -- 5 wide-binary pairs share one HIP
  number and hip_name string) instead of a stable per-row ID. Fixed by
  introducing `target_id` (assigned once at load time) and merging on it
  instead; regenerated all three result files; added a regression test
  (`test_combined_view_does_not_fan_out_on_shared_hip_name`). The same
  review found that `evidence_completeness` previously included two
  archive fields (`sy_planets_flag`/`sy_disks_flag` populated) that are
  populated for essentially every row regardless of real observational
  coverage, silently inflating every star's score; fixed by excluding them
  (claim 9 above) and renamed `find_undisclosed_companion_conflicts` to
  `find_hgca_accelerators_without_archive_listed_planets` to stop implying
  a confirmed catalog disagreement (claim 5 above). See `CHANGELOG.md` for
  the full list of fixes in this release.
