## Data and methods

- The target list is the Direct Imaging ExEP Mission Star List (164 stars),
  published by Mamajek & Stapelfeldt (2024, ApJS 272, 32) and hosted by the
  NASA Exoplanet Archive (DOI 10.26133/NEA39); see `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`.
- Astrometric acceleration evidence is drawn from the Hipparcos-Gaia
  Catalog of Accelerations (Brandt 2021, ApJS 254, 42), accessed via
  VizieR (CDS, Strasbourg).
- This project is an independent cross-match and is not an official Habitable
  Worlds Observatory, ExEP, or NASA Exoplanet Archive product, and is not
  affiliated with or endorsed by NASA, STScI, JPL, or the ExEP program.
